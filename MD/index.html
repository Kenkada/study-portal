Mechanical Design: the course

Short lessons that follow your syllabus unit by unit, built on V B Bhandari's Design of Machine Elements (3rd ed.). Each has derivations, worked Bhandari examples, instant-feedback practice, and the exact page to read in the book and in your professor's slides.

How to use it. Do one lesson at a sitting (20–40 min). Answer the warm-up from memory before peeking, try each step of a worked example before revealing it, and type an answer into every practice box. Come back to the unit's mixed-practice lesson a few days later: spaced, mixed practice is what makes it stick for the exam. Print the formula sheets for revision.

Book and slide references give the exact page. The PDFs aren't published online, so on the website they are plain page numbers. Opened from the course folder on your own computer, they link straight to the PDFs. Equations need an internet connection the first time (KaTeX).
