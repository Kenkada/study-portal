Design basics, materials and fatigue: formula and procedure sheet

Notation follows Bhandari. N, mm, N/mm² (= MPa) throughout. Prints on 2–3 pages.

Allowable stress and factor of safety

Allowable stress (§4.2)

\[ \sigma = \tfrac{S_{yt}}{(fs)}\ \text{ductile} \quad \sigma = \tfrac{S_{ut}}{(fs)}\ \text{brittle} \quad \tau = \tfrac{S_{sy}}{(fs)} \]

Ssy = 0.5 Syt (MSST) or 0.577 Syt (DET). FG 200 means Sut = 200.

Typical (fs) (Bhandari pp. 78–79)

Cast iron 3–5 on Sut · ductile static 1.5–2 on Syt · fatigue 1.3–1.5 on Se · contact 1.8–2.5 · buckling 3–6 · simple parts up to 5.

Stresses (Lesson 1)

Basic

\[ \sigma=\tfrac{P}{A},\ \ \tau=\tfrac{P}{A},\ \ \delta=\tfrac{Pl}{AE},\ \ \sigma_b=\tfrac{M_by}{I},\ \ \tau=\tfrac{M_tr}{J} \]

Solid round: σb = 32M/πd³, τ = 16T/πd³. Irect = bd³/12; I = πd⁴/64; J = πd⁴/32; hollow J = 2I.

Torque, twist, eccentric load

\[ M_t = \tfrac{60\times10^6\,kW}{2\pi n}\ (\text{N·mm}) \quad \theta^\circ = \tfrac{584M_tl}{Gd^4} \quad \sigma = \tfrac{P}{A}\pm\tfrac{Pey}{I} \]

Steel E = 207 000, G = 80 000; CI E = 100 000, G = 40 000.

Impact (§5.16)

\[ \sigma_i=\tfrac{W}{A}\Big[1+\sqrt{1+\tfrac{2hAE}{Wl}}\Big] \]

Suddenly applied (h = 0): σ = 2W/A. Modulus of resilience u = Syt²/2E.

Principal stresses and failure theories (Lesson 2)

Principal stresses

\[ \sigma_{1,2}=\tfrac{\sigma_x+\sigma_y}{2}\pm\sqrt{\big(\tfrac{\sigma_x-\sigma_y}{2}\big)^2+\tau^2},\quad \tau_{max}=\sqrt{\big(\tfrac{\sigma_x-\sigma_y}{2}\big)^2+\tau^2} \]

Shaft with Mb and Mt

\[ \text{MPST: } \tfrac{16}{\pi d^3}\big(M_b+\sqrt{M_b^2+M_t^2}\big)=\tfrac{S}{(fs)} \] \[ \text{MSST: } \tfrac{16}{\pi d^3}\sqrt{M_b^2+M_t^2}=\tfrac{0.5S_{yt}}{(fs)} \] \[ \text{DET: } \tfrac{16}{\pi d^3}\sqrt{4M_b^2+3M_t^2}=\tfrac{S_{yt}}{(fs)} \]

Choosing

Brittle → MPST. Ductile → MSST (easy, conservative, hexagon inside ellipse) or DET (accurate). DET for σ + τ: \(\sqrt{\sigma^2+3\tau^2}\). Biaxial: \(\sqrt{\sigma_1^2-\sigma_1\sigma_2+\sigma_2^2}\).

Design process, criteria, materials (Lessons 3–5)

Design process (syllabus wording)

Need → analysis (specs) → conceptual design (alternatives) → technical viability → economic viability → financial viability → preliminary → detailed design → drawings. Shigley phases: need, definition, synthesis, analysis and optimisation, evaluation, presentation.

Design criteria

Strength σ ≤ S/(fs) · Stiffness δ, θ ≤ limit (E, G govern) · Resilience elastic energy, u = Syt²/2E · Production and maintenance: DFMA, standard parts, accessibility.

Preferred numbers

Fatigue (Lessons 6–10)

Cyclic stress

\[ \sigma_m=\tfrac12(\sigma_{max}+\sigma_{min})\quad \sigma_a=\tfrac12(\sigma_{max}-\sigma_{min}) \]

Tension +, compression −. Repeated: σmin = 0. Reversed: σm = 0.

Endurance limit

\[ S_e = K_aK_bK_cK_d\,S'_e,\quad S'_e=0.5S_{ut}\ (\text{steel}) \]

0.4Sut cast iron, cast steel, wrought Al; 0.3 cast Al. Kb: ≤ 7.5 mm 1; ≤ 50 mm 0.85; > 50 mm 0.75 (axial 1). Kc: 50% 1, 90% 0.897, 95% 0.868, 99% 0.814, 99.9% 0.753. Ka = 1 for polished and for grey CI. Axial (Se)a = 0.8Se; torsion Sse = 0.5 or 0.577 Se.

Stress concentration

\[ K_t=\tfrac{\sigma_{max}}{\sigma_o},\ \ K_f=1+q(K_t-1),\ \ K_d=\tfrac1{K_f} \]

Ductile static: ignore. Ductile fatigue: use Kf. Brittle: always Kt. Circular hole Kt = 3; elliptical 1 + 2a/b; keyway (bending + torsion) Kt = 3.

Reversed stress (§5.10)

Infinite: σa = Se/(fs); τa = Sse/(fs).

\[ \log S_f=\log(0.9S_{ut})-\tfrac{\log(0.9S_{ut})-\log S_e}{3}(\log N-3) \]

Miner: Σ ni/Ni = 1, or 1/N = Σ αi/Ni.

Fluctuating stress (§5.12–5.14)

\[ \tfrac{S_m}{S_{yt}}+\tfrac{S_a}{S_e}=1\ \text{(Soderberg)} \quad \tfrac{S_m}{S_{ut}}+\tfrac{S_a}{S_e}=1\ \text{(Goodman)} \] \[ \tfrac{S_a}{S_e}+\big(\tfrac{S_m}{S_{ut}}\big)^2=1\ \text{(Gerber)} \]

tan θ = σa/σm = Pa/Pm. Goodman ∩: Sa = 1/(1/Se + 1/(Suttanθ)). Check Sa + Sm ≤ Syt. Design σa = Sa/(fs). Torsion: τa = Sse/(fs), τmax ≤ Ssy/(fs). Finite life with mean: Sf = σaSut/(Sut − σm).

Combined (§5.15) and Soderberg equivalents

\[ \sigma_m=\sqrt{\sigma_{xm}^2+3\tau_m^2},\ \ \sigma_a=\sqrt{\sigma_{xa}^2+3\tau_a^2} \]

Then the Goodman diagram. Slides' method: \(\sigma_{eq}=\sigma_m+\sigma_a\frac{S_{yt}}{S_e}\frac{K_f}{K_{sur}K_{sz}}\), same for τ; then \(\frac{16}{\pi d^3}\sqrt{M_{eq}^2+T_{eq}^2}=\frac{S_{sy}}{(fs)}\).
