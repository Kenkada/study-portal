AMP glossary: Unit 3

The words every lesson uses, defined once. Use these exact terms in answers; examiners reward precise vocabulary.

Spark processes (EDM, Wire EDM)

Inter-electrode gap (IEG)
The small dielectric-filled gap between tool and workpiece across which sparks occur, typically 12–50 µm in operation.
Avoid: spark distance, clearance
Dielectric
The insulating liquid in the IEG that breaks down to form the spark channel, then deionises, cools and flushes. Kerosene, EDM oil, deionised water.
Avoid: coolant, electrolyte (that is ECM)
Breakdown voltage (Vb)
Gap voltage at which the dielectric ionises and a spark forms. For maximum power in an RC circuit, Vb ≈ 0.72 V0.
Ignition delay
Time between voltage application and dielectric breakdown.
Normal (straight) polarity
Tool negative, workpiece positive. Reverse polarity: tool positive, workpiece negative.
Pulse on-time, off-time, duty cycle
On-time: spark duration. Off-time: pause for deionisation and flushing. Duty cycle = ton / (ton + toff).
Relaxation (RC) generator
Power supply in which a capacitor charges through a resistor and discharges across the gap, giving a sawtooth voltage.
Critical resistance
Smallest charging resistance that still lets the RC circuit relax into discrete sparks; below it a continuous arc forms.
Arc
A sustained, uncontrolled discharge at one spot; damages tool and work. Caused by debris or too-low resistance.
Wear ratio
Volume of tool eroded ÷ volume of work eroded (Jain's definition). Relative electrode wear (REW) is the same, expressed in %.
State the direction you use; some texts invert it.
Overcut
Radial clearance between tool and machined feature, roughly equal to the spark gap. Diametral overcut is twice this.
Flushing
Forcing clean dielectric through the gap to remove debris: pressure, suction, side/jet, or pulsed with electrode retraction.
Recast (white) layer
Melt that refroze on the machined surface: hard, brittle, porous, microcracked.
Heat-affected zone (HAZ)
Layer below the surface that was heated but not melted, with altered properties and residual stress.
Converted layer
Zone below the HAZ where grain structure has changed from the parent metal.
Electrode refeeding
Returning the tool to a reference point after each hole so length lost to wear does not make later holes shallower.
Kerf
Width of material removed by a cut: wire diameter plus twice the overcut in Wire EDM.
Stratified wire
Copper-core wire with a zinc coating that vaporises first, keeping the core cool so the wire carries more energy.
Wire bowing
Sideways bending of the wire by gas-bubble pressure; causes lag at corners. Reduced by higher tension.
U–V axes
Extra CNC axes that move the upper wire guide to tilt the wire for taper cutting.

Beam and plasma processes (LBM, EBM, PAM, IBM)

Stimulated emission
Emission of a photon identical to a passing photon by an excited atom; the basis of the laser.
Population inversion
State in which more atoms occupy a higher energy level than a lower one, so stimulated emission outpaces absorption.
Pumping
Supplying energy to create population inversion: flash lamp (optical), electrical discharge, or atom–atom collision.
Resonator (optical cavity)
Fully and partially reflecting mirrors that feed photons back through the medium; the partial mirror lets the beam out.
Power density (intensity)
Beam power ÷ spot area, W/cm². Decides whether a beam heats, welds or machines.
Beam divergence (θ)
Angular spread of a beam; focused spot diameter W = fθ.
Depth of focus
Length along the beam over which the waist diameter stays nearly constant; larger for longer focal length.
Assist gas
Gas jet coaxial with a laser beam that clears melt and protects the lens; O₂ adds exothermic heat, Ar/N₂ give clean edges.
Trepanning
Making a large hole by tracing its outline with the beam (or electrode) instead of removing the whole volume.
Thermionic emission
Release of electrons from a hot cathode; the electron source in an EBM gun.
Grid (bias) cup
Negatively biased electrode in the EBM gun that controls beam current and switches the beam into pulses.
Backing material
Sacrificial layer under the EBM workpiece that vaporises at breakthrough and blows molten metal out of the hole.
Specific energy (W)
Energy needed to remove unit volume. For EBM: W = ρ[cs(Tm − Ti) + Lf + cl(Tb − Tm) + Lv].
Plasma
Ionised gas of free electrons, ions and neutral atoms; electrically conductive; the "fourth state of matter".
Transferred arc
Plasma arc running from cathode to the (conductive) workpiece; high efficiency; used for cutting.
Non-transferred arc
Plasma arc running from cathode to the nozzle; the work is outside the circuit, so it may be non-conductive.
Double arcing
Arc jumping electrode → nozzle → workpiece; destroys nozzles in air plasma torches.
Stand-off distance (SOD)
Distance from nozzle tip to work surface.
Sputtering
Ejection of surface atoms by momentum transfer from impinging ions; IBM's removal mechanism. No melting.
Sputter yield (S)
Average number of target atoms ejected per incident ion.
Neutraliser
Electron source that neutralises the ion beam so insulating targets do not charge up.
