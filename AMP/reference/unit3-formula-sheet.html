EDM: RC relaxation circuit (Jain Eqs 7.1–7.17)

\[ V_{ct} = V_0\left(1 - e^{-t/R_cC}\right), \qquad i_{ct} = \frac{V_0}{R_c}e^{-t/R_cC}, \qquad \tau = R_cC \;(\text{63.2\% of } V_0) \]
\[ E = \frac{V_0^2\tau}{R_c}\left[\tfrac12 + \tfrac12 e^{-2t/\tau} - e^{-t/\tau}\right] = \tfrac12 C V_{ct}^2, \qquad P_{avg} = \frac{V_0^2}{R_c x}\left[\tfrac12 + \tfrac12 e^{-2x} - e^{-x}\right],\; x = \frac{\tau_c}{\tau} \]
\[ \frac{dP_{avg}}{dx} = 0 \Rightarrow x \approx 1.26 \Rightarrow V_b = V_0(1 - e^{-1.26}) \approx 0.72\,V_0 \]
\[ \text{Spark energy } W_d = \tfrac12 C V_b^2, \qquad \text{discharge: } V = V_{co}e^{-t/R_sC} \]
\[ t_c = R_cC\ln\frac{1}{1 - V_b/V_0}, \qquad f_c = \frac{1}{t_c}, \qquad \text{MRR} = \frac{K_4 V_b^2}{R_c \ln\!\left(\frac{1}{1 - V_b/V_0}\right)} \;\propto\; \frac{1}{R_c} \]
\[ R_{crit} = \sqrt{L/C}\;(\text{theory}), \quad R_{crit} \ge 30\sqrt{L/C}\;(\text{experiment}) \]
\[ h \propto C^{1/3}V_b^{2/3}, \quad H \propto \frac{h}{f_c}, \quad H = K_6 V_b^{2/3} C^{n+1/3} R^m, \quad \textbf{experimental: } H = K_7 V_b^{0.5} C^{0.31\text{–}0.36} \]

EDM: pulse generator and general

\[ \text{Duty} = \frac{t_{on}}{t_{on}+t_{off}}, \quad P_{avg} = VI\times\text{duty}, \quad E_{pulse} = V I t_{on}, \quad f = \frac{1}{t_{on}+t_{off}} \]
\[ \text{MRR} = \frac{\eta P}{\rho\,u}\;(u \text{ in J/kg}), \quad u_{vol} = \frac{P}{\text{MRR}}\;(\text{J/mm}^3), \quad \text{Wear ratio} = \frac{V_{tool}}{V_{work}}, \quad \text{overcut} = \frac{D_{hole} - D_{tool}}{2} \]
\[ \text{Crater (hemisphere): } \tfrac23\pi r^3 = \frac{\eta E}{\rho\,u}, \qquad R_a \approx k\sqrt{E_{pulse}} \]

Wire EDM

\[ b = d_w + 2\,\text{overcut}, \quad \text{MRR} = b\,t\,v_f, \quad \text{area rate} = t\,v_f, \quad \tan\theta = \frac{(b_{top} - b_{bottom})/2}{t} \]

LBM

\[ I = \frac{P}{\pi d^2/4}, \quad P_{peak} = \frac{E_p}{t_p}, \quad E_p = \frac{P_{avg}}{f_{rep}}, \quad W = f\theta, \quad \delta \approx \sqrt{\alpha t}, \quad P = \frac{\rho\,\text{MRR}\,u}{\eta} \]

Machining needs about 1.5 × 10⁶ – 1.5 × 10⁸ W/cm²; welding 1.5 × 10⁴ – 1.5 × 10⁵ W/cm² (Jain p.190).

EBM (Jain Eqs 10.1–10.2)

\[ \text{MRR} = \frac{\eta P}{W}, \qquad W = \rho\left[c_s(T_m - T_i) + L_f + c_l(T_b - T_m) + L_v\right], \qquad P = V I \]
\[ \text{Penetration per pulse} = \frac{\eta P t_p}{\rho L}\Big/ \frac{\pi d^2}{4} \]

PAM

\[ S\,(\text{m/min}) = \frac{25.4}{t\,(\text{mm})}\;\text{at }500\text{ A (Jain 9.1)}, \qquad v = \frac{kP}{t}, \qquad P_{gas} = \dot m c_p \Delta T, \qquad v = \frac{\eta P}{u_{vol}\,b\,t} \]

IBM

\[ \dot h = \frac{J\,S\,M}{\rho\,F} \quad (J\text{ A/m}^2,\ S\text{ atoms/ion},\ M\text{ kg/mol},\ \rho\text{ kg/m}^3,\ F = 96{,}485) \]

Errata in the source books

Rc = 4.15 Ω (printed 6.02 Ω).
